# 🛍️ Creator Monetization Studio Guide

LinkForge empowers creators to transform their link-in-bio into a high-converting digital storefront with **zero platform cuts, direct creator payouts, and instant digital fulfillment**.

---

## 🌟 Monetization Offerings

### 1. 🎓 Paid Courses & Masterclasses
* **Curriculum Syllabus Builder**: Create multi-chapter courses with structured modules (e.g. Chapter 1: Introduction, Chapter 2: Deep Dive).
* **Lesson Duration Badges**: Display estimated completion times per lesson (`15m`, `45m`).
* **Sample Previews**: Tag lessons as `Free Preview` vs `Locked for Enrolled Students`.
* **Dynamic Pricing**: High-contrast discount pricing (e.g. `₹1,499` slashed from `₹4,999`).

### 2. 🤝 Paid 1:1 Mentorship & Consultation Calls
* Offer advisory sessions, code audits, architecture reviews, and mock interviews.
* **Duration Controls**: Offer flexible session lengths (`30m`, `45m`, `60m`).
* **Direct Calendar Integration**: Embed scheduling links directly to Cal.com, Calendly, Google Meet, or custom booking URLs.

### 3. 📦 Digital Downloads & Resource Bundles
* Sell digital assets directly from your profile: PDF engineering guides, UI kits, design systems, and source code ZIP archives.
* **Instant Delivery**: Download buttons and automated fulfillment links presented immediately after purchase confirmation.

### 4. 💳 0% Fee Direct Payments & Global Creator Tipping
* **Direct UPI Payment Cards**: Accept instant peer-to-peer payments via QR code (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) with **0% platform intermediary commission**.
* **Global Creator Support**: Direct tip jars and membership links (Stripe, PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee).
